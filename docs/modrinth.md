# Spice of Life: Revival

**Variety is the spice of life.**

Steak again? Your stomach is getting tired of it.

Spice of Life: Revival brings the classic diminishing-returns diet back to modern Minecraft. Every meal you repeat fills you up a little less, and takes a little longer to get down. Eat something different, and your old favourite tastes great again.

## How it tastes

- 🍖 **Seconds are fine.** It is the third helping that starts to wear thin.
- 🐌 **Boring food is slow food.** The less a food is worth, the longer it takes to eat.
- 🔄 **Your stomach forgets.** Only your last 12 meals count. Mix things up and everything comes back.
- 🎂 **Cake counts too.** One slice at a time.

| Times in your last 12 meals | 0–1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value | 100% | 75% | 50% | 25% | 0% |

## Plays well with others

- **AppleSkin**: its previews show what a food is really worth to you right now.
- **Server-side**: drop it on a server and it just works. Add it to clients for slower eating, tooltips and AppleSkin previews.
- **Fabric and NeoForge**, translated into 14 languages.
- **Make it your own**: history length, the value table, eating speed, what happens on death and foods that never get boring are all in `config/solrevival.properties`. See [GitHub](https://github.com/yuuki14202028/SpiceOfLifeRevival) for the details.

No food groups. No journals. No extra items. Just a reason to plant that carrot farm.
